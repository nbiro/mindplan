# Browse the graph

## Purpose

Lets an agent look across the plan rather than at one node: the whole graph a page at a time (so no result is silently cut), the areas with their member counts (cheap enough to call before orienting), and the decisions of a node, a change, or the whole graph.

## Actor & Trigger

Agent calls `get_mindplan_graph` to see the plan's structure, `list_areas` when unsure where work lands, and `list_decisions` before reshaping a design someone else chose.

## PRD / Execution Logic

Intended files: `src/interactions/i-browse-graph/` (`handlers.ts`, `tools.ts`).

- `get_mindplan_graph({ offset?, limit? })` pages nodes (default `GRAPH_PAGE_DEFAULT` 200, max `GRAPH_PAGE_MAX` 500) and returns the edges whose source is in the page, the totals, and `next_offset` while more remain
- `list_areas()` returns `{ columns, rows, count }`, a row per active Journey: id, title, description cut to 60 characters, and counts of its active Interactions, the Interfaces exposing them, the Foundations they depend on, and open Bugs
- `list_decisions({ node_id? | change? , include_superseded? })` returns `{ decisions, count }` for one node, one change, or the whole graph, each with node, status, and `superseded_by`; superseded entries only with `include_superseded`
- Reads; no orientation gate

Depends on: `f-graph-search`, `f-territory-store`, `f-compiler-rules`, `f-domain-model`, `f-tool-kit`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `get_mindplan_graph` on a graph larger than `limit` returns `limit` nodes, only edges from those nodes, the totals, and `next_offset`; walking every page yields every node and edge exactly once; `limit` above 500 is rejected
- `list_areas` lists 30 areas under 4 KB with member and open-Bug counts
- `list_decisions` returns a node's, a change's, and the graph's decisions; with `include_superseded` the superseded entry carries `superseded_by`
- Verified via `npm test`

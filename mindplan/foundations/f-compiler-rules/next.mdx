# Compiler rules (impact section shape)

## Purpose

Infra — MindPlan compiler. Keep the shipped gates: Completion Check, Open Checklist While Building, Minimum Territory Shape, Interaction Independence, Infrastructure First, Behavior First, cancel gates, force_unship, reachability, non-empty existing `implements` on `in-review` and `ship`, and the import-matrix closure that stops at assemblers.

### d-established-equivalents-anchor

- Decision: SPEC names, for each type and edge, its closest established architecture term and where MindPlan differs from it, as a one-time anchor; the type names stay the only words used.
- Alternatives:
  - A plain-word glossary (removed in 0.16.0): it offered other words to use, and agents used them instead of the type names
  - No mapping: agents meet five unfamiliar names and rebuild their meaning from rules alone, when they already know use cases, adapters, and business capabilities
- Why: Agents and people map a new name onto a concept they already know; saying which concept, and where it differs, gives them that prior without making the known term a synonym.
- Consequences: The equivalents table is non-normative, and README, playbook, and skills still MUST NOT use the established terms as synonyms for a type.
- Change: capability-type
- Date: 2026-11-01
